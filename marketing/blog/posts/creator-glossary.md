This creator glossary defines the terms that get used loosely in creator conversations,
precisely enough that two people using them mean the same thing. Most disagreements about
performance turn out to be disagreements about definitions.

## Creator glossary: audience and reach

**Followers / subscribers.** People who opted in. A threshold metric rather than a measure
of health — [vanity metrics](/blog/vanity-metrics).

**Reach.** The number of unique accounts that saw a post. Always smaller than followers for
most accounts.

**Impressions.** The number of times a post was displayed, including repeats. Always
equal to or larger than reach.

**Views.** Platform-specific and inconsistent. Some count a fraction of a second, others
require several seconds. Never compare view counts across platforms.

## Engagement

**Engagement rate.** Interactions divided by an audience number, times 100. Which audience
number changes the answer several-fold —
[engagement rate](/blog/engagement-rate) and
[by reach vs by followers](/blog/engagement-rate-by-reach-vs-followers).

[[tool:engagement-rate-calculator]]

**Interactions.** Likes, comments, saves, shares. Which of these you can see depends on the
platform and on whether the account is yours.

**Saves.** The strongest signal on most platforms, because a save implies future intent.

**Completion rate.** The share of viewers who watched to the end. The dominant signal in
short-form.

**Retention.** The curve of how many viewers remain over a video's length. The dominant
signal in long-form.

**Watch time.** Total time watched. Retention × length × views, and the currency of
YouTube's Partner Programme —
[YouTube monetization requirements](/blog/youtube-monetization-requirements).

## Money

**CPM.** Cost per mille — what an advertiser pays per 1,000 ad impressions, before the
platform's share.

**RPM.** Revenue per mille — what you earn per 1,000 video views, after the platform's
share and including non-ad income. Always lower than CPM. See
[RPM vs CPM](/blog/rpm-vs-cpm).

[[tool:youtube-money-calculator]]

**Monetized playbacks.** Views where an ad actually ran. Fewer than total views.

**Revenue share.** The platform's split. YouTube pays 55% of net ad revenue on long-form
watch-page ads and 45% of the Shorts pool allocated by view share
([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72902)).

**Rate card.** What you charge brands. Negotiated on audience fit rather than calculated
from a ratio — [social media benchmarks](/blog/social-media-benchmarks).

## Discovery

**CTR.** Click-through rate — of the people shown your thumbnail or link, how many clicked.

**Impressions-to-click.** The same thing, phrased to make the denominator explicit.

**Suggested / recommended.** Distribution the platform chooses, as opposed to search or
following.

**SEO.** On a platform, optimising the fields the ranking system reads —
[YouTube SEO](/blog/youtube-seo) and [Pinterest SEO](/blog/pinterest-seo).

## Business terms

**Niche.** The subject an account is recognisably about. The single largest determinant of
what advertisers pay - [how much do YouTubers make](/blog/how-much-do-youtubers-make).

**Pillar.** One of the three or four recurring subjects a content plan rotates between -
[a content calendar that survives a week](/blog/content-calendar-guide).

**Funnel.** The path from someone seeing you to someone doing something. On social it is
usually: short-form reach, profile visit, follow, and eventually something off-platform.

**Owned audience.** People you can reach without a platform's permission - an email list,
mostly. The only audience that survives an algorithm change.

**Repurposing.** Reshaping one piece of work for several formats and platforms -
[content repurposing](/blog/content-repurposing).

**UTM parameters.** Labels on a URL that tell your analytics where a visitor came from -
[UTM parameters for creators](/blog/utm-parameters-for-creators).

## Format terms

**Safe zone.** The part of a frame not covered by interface —
[safe zones](/blog/social-media-safe-zones).

**Aspect ratio.** The shape of a frame, independent of resolution —
[aspect ratio](/blog/aspect-ratios-explained).

**The fold.** Where text is truncated behind "more". Different on every platform.

**Open Graph.** The metadata that produces link previews —
[Open Graph tags](/blog/open-graph-tags).

:::faq
Q: What is the difference between reach and impressions?
A: Reach counts unique accounts that saw a post; impressions count displays including
repeats. Impressions are always equal to or higher.
Q: What is the difference between RPM and CPM?
A: CPM is what advertisers pay per 1,000 ad impressions before the platform's cut. RPM is
your revenue per 1,000 views after it.
Q: Is a view the same on every platform?
A: No. The threshold for counting a view differs by platform, which makes cross-platform
view comparisons meaningless.
Q: What is completion rate?
A: The share of viewers who watched a video to the end - the dominant ranking signal in
short-form video.
:::
