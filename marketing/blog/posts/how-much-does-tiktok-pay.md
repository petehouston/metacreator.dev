How much does TikTok pay? Through a rewards programme calculated per 1,000 qualified
views - and the rate varies enormously — by region, by video length, by whether the views
qualify at all. Anyone quoting a single figure is quoting their own account on their own
week.

## How much TikTok pays: the calculation

The mechanism, rather than a number:

- **Qualified views only.** Views below a minimum watch duration, and views from regions
  outside the programme, do not count.
- **Longer videos qualify.** The rewards programme is built around videos over a minimum
  length — short clips are excluded from it entirely.
- **Region matters more than anything.** Advertising markets differ by many multiples,
  and payouts follow them.
- **Originality requirements.** Reposted, spliced or low-effort content is excluded.

## Why the numbers you have seen disagree

Every creator quoting a per-thousand rate is describing a different combination of the
variables above. Two creators with a million views each can be paid very differently
because one has a US audience and the other does not, or because one made 90-second
videos and the other made 20-second ones.

There is a second reason the figures conflict: the programmes change. TikTok has
restructured creator payments more than once, and old articles describing an earlier
scheme are still ranking. This page deliberately describes the mechanism rather than a
rate, because the mechanism is what stays true — check the current terms in TikTok's own
[Creator Portal](https://www.tiktok.com/creators/creator-portal/).

## Where TikTok money actually comes from

For almost every creator earning meaningfully from TikTok, the platform's own payments
are the smallest line:

| Stream | Depends on | Typically |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rewards programme | Views, region, length | Small |
| Brand deals | Audience fit and engagement | The largest line |
| Affiliate and shop commission | Whether the audience buys | Can exceed everything else |
| Your own product or service | Whether you have one | No ceiling |
| Live gifts | A live habit and a warm audience | Variable |

The practical consequence is the same as on YouTube: platform payouts are a bonus, and
the audience is the asset. What a brand pays is negotiated on fit and engagement rather
than on a rate card —

## What qualifies, and what quietly does not

The word doing the most work in "per 1,000 qualified views" is *qualified*, and it
excludes more than people expect.

**Views that end quickly.** A swipe-past is a view in your analytics and not a qualified
one for payment. This is why a video with a huge view count and poor retention can pay
almost nothing.

**Views from outside eligible regions.** Payments are region-bound, and a video that
travels internationally accrues views that count for reach and not for revenue.

**Videos under the minimum length.** Short clips are outside the rewards programme
entirely, whatever they do.

**Anything judged unoriginal.** Reposts, compilations and lightly edited third-party
footage are excluded, and the exclusion is applied after the fact rather than announced
in advance.

The result is a gap between what your analytics show and what appears in the payments
screen, and that gap is the source of most of the confusion about TikTok rates. It is
not an error; it is the difference between two things called "views".

## How this compares to YouTube

Worth knowing if you are choosing where to put effort. YouTube pays 55% of net ad revenue
on long-form watch-page ads and 45% of the pooled Shorts revenue allocated by view share
([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72902)). Long-form YouTube pays
dramatically more per view than any short-form programme, TikTok's included.

That is the honest strategic picture: short-form is for reach, long-form is for revenue.

## Getting there at all

Reach comes first, and on TikTok reach is available to accounts with no following —
which is the platform's real advantage. What earns it is in
[TikTok growth](/blog/tiktok-growth-guide).

:::faq
Q: How much does TikTok pay per 1,000 views?
A: It varies by region, video length and whether views qualify. There is no single rate,
and any figure quoted without those variables describes one account.
Q: Does TikTok pay for short videos?
A: The rewards programme is built around longer videos and excludes very short clips
from qualifying entirely.
Q: Is TikTok or YouTube better for money?
A: YouTube long-form pays far more per view. TikTok is better for reach, particularly for
an account with no audience yet.
Q: How do most TikTok creators earn?
A: Brand deals, affiliate and shop commission, and their own products. Platform payouts
are usually the smallest line.
:::
