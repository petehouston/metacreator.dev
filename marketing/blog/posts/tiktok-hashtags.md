Use three to five TikTok hashtags, all specific to what the video is actually about.
Hashtags on TikTok are a topic signal that helps the system categorise a video — they
are not a reach multiplier, and the highest-volume tags are the least useful ones you
can pick.

## Why #fyp does nothing

`#fyp`, `#foryou` and `#viral` appear on a colossal number of videos, including
everything from every niche. As a signal they say nothing: a tag that describes every
video describes none.

They also cost you. The caption is shared between your copy and your tags, and it sits
on top of your frame — every character spent on a tag that carries no information is a
character not spent on a hook, and more caption lines means more of your video covered.

## What a specific tag does

TikTok's recommendation system decides who to show a video to, and it needs to
understand the video first. Hashtags are one of several inputs — alongside the audio,
the on-screen text, the caption and what happens when the first few hundred people see
it.

A tag like `#homeespresso` narrows the initial audience to people who have shown
interest in that subject. A narrower audience that watches to the end is a far better
outcome than a broad audience that swipes away, because completion is what the system
optimises for.

That is the counter-intuitive part: **narrowing your tags usually increases reach**,
because the first audience is better matched.

## Building a TikTok hashtags set

| Type | How many | Example shape |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Subject | 2 | What the video is literally about |
| Niche or community | 1-2 | The group that cares about it |
| Format or series | 0-1 | Your own recurring tag |
| Generic reach tags | 0 | None. They do nothing |

Three to five total. If a sixth tag is genuinely descriptive, it is fine — the number
is not sacred, the relevance is.

## Trending tags and trending sounds

A genuinely trending tag can help if the video actually belongs to that trend. Joining a
trend your video has nothing to do with produces the same mismatch as `#fyp`: a poorly
matched initial audience that swipes away.

The stronger trend signal on TikTok has never been the hashtag anyway — it is the audio.
A video using a rising sound is categorised and distributed partly on that basis, and it
is a better-aligned signal than a tag anyone can type.

## What actually drives distribution

In rough order of weight:

1. **The first two seconds.** Whether people stay at all.
2. **Completion and rewatches.** The strongest positive signal there is.
3. **Shares and comments.** Engagement that implies the video was worth passing on.
4. **Topic signals** — audio, caption, on-screen text, and hashtags.

Hashtags are in that list, at the bottom. A well-tagged video that loses people in two
seconds goes nowhere; a badly tagged video that holds attention finds its audience
anyway. The fuller picture is in
[TikTok growth](/blog/tiktok-growth-guide), and TikTok's own guidance lives in its
[Creator Portal](https://www.tiktok.com/creators/creator-portal/).

## Not the same as Instagram or YouTube

None of the above transfers. Instagram allows 30 and treats tags differently; YouTube
shows three above the title and ignores every tag past 60. The cross-network comparison
is [how to use hashtags](/blog/how-to-use-hashtags), and the Instagram specifics are in
[Instagram hashtags](/blog/instagram-hashtags).

:::faq
Q: How many hashtags should I use on TikTok?
A: Three to five, all describing what the video is actually about. There is no benefit
to filling the caption with tags.
Q: Does #fyp work on TikTok?
A: No. It appears on an enormous number of unrelated videos, so it carries no
information about yours - and it uses caption space that covers your frame.
Q: Do TikTok hashtags affect the For You page?
A: They are one topic signal among several. Watch time, completion and shares carry far
more weight.
Q: Should I use trending hashtags?
A: Only when the video genuinely belongs to that trend. A mismatch sends the video to
people who will swipe away, which hurts rather than helps.
:::
